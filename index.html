Unit economics

CAC, Cost of ARR, Payback, and LTV : CAC

Four metrics, one company, and four different answers to “which segment should we fund?” A constructed case — Meridian Systems, three go-to-market motions — engineered so the metrics produce three different winners from the same seven inputs. The point is not the arithmetic but the blind spots: which single piece of information each metric holds that the others structurally cannot see.

Reading a metric by its units rather than its name; separating what is measured from what is assumed; and knowing which question each number is actually competent to answer.
